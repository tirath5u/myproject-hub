# myproduct-hub - Personal Tools Landing Page

A minimal, clean white and light-maroon landing page for **myproduct.life**, introducing Tirath Chhatriwala and showcasing his suite of PM tools.

## Design Direction

- **Palette:** White background, deep charcoal text, and light maroon as the single accent for links, hover states, and tile borders.
- **Type:** System sans, generous line-height, large calm headings, and Stripe/Linear-style restraint.
- **Layout:** Centered max-width container around 1100px, heavy whitespace, subtle dividers, and responsive single-column to three-column grids.

## Routes

Single-page landing. All sections live on `/` because they are tightly connected and short.

- `src/routes/index.tsx`: full landing page with all sections.
- `src/routes/__root.tsx`: site shell, shared meta, and route outlet.
- `src/routeTree.gen.ts`: generated by TanStack Router.

## Page Sections

1. Header: `myproduct.life` wordmark plus Tools, Strategy, Writing, and Contact anchors.
2. Hero: product manager positioning and two CTAs.
3. My Tools: live Project SOR and COD Updates cards plus coming-soon tools.
4. My Strategy: empty state for frameworks and strategy notes.
5. My Writing: empty state for essays.
6. Footer: bio line, copyright, and placeholder social links.

## Technical Notes

- Tailwind v4 theme tokens map semantic colors through `src/styles.css`.
- Smooth scroll behavior is enabled for anchor nav.
- All content is authored as real text so the page ships ready.
- The COD Updates card links to `https://cod.myproduct.life`.
